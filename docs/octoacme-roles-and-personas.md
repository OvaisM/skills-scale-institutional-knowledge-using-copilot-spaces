# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors champion the business outcomes of a project and provide the organizational support needed to achieve them.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and champion the intended business outcomes
- Secure resources and remove organizational barriers
- Make or escalate decisions about scope, priority, and major trade-offs
- Review progress against expected outcomes and resolve escalated issues

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers meaningful business value
- Keep leadership aligned on progress, decisions, and risks

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to align outcomes and priorities
- Work with the Project Manager on resources, milestones, escalations, and stakeholder updates
- Provide timely decisions to Developers and other delivery roles through the Project Manager and Product Manager

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and sponsor check-ins
- Escalations and decisions with the Project Manager and Product Manager

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into actionable, verifiable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements, workflows, and constraints
- Break down requirements into clear scope and acceptance criteria
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies with stakeholders
- Support validation that delivered work meets the agreed requirements

### Goals
- Make project needs understandable and testable
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or incomplete requirements

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to refine backlog items and confirm alignment with product outcomes
- Coordinate with Stakeholders to clarify needs and resolve conflicting requirements
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to make requirements feasible and acceptance criteria verifiable
- Keep the Project Manager informed about scope questions and dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and process documentation
- Backlog refinement and acceptance-criteria discussions

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers identify user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and share relevant findings
- Design user flows, interaction patterns, and interface concepts
- Consider accessibility and usability throughout design
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback

### Goals
- Ensure delivered experiences solve real user problems
- Make products clear, accessible, and easy to use

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to connect user needs and research findings to product priorities
- Coordinate with Stakeholders to understand user and business context
- Partner with Developers to assess design feasibility and implementation details
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility expectations in validation

### Typical Communication
- Research findings, design artifacts, and usability reviews
- Design handoffs and feedback sessions with Developers and the Product Manager

---

## Technical Leads and Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Solution Architects guide technical decisions and help ensure the solution is maintainable, reliable, and compatible with connected systems.

### Responsibilities
- Define or guide technical designs, architecture, and engineering standards
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support Developers with complex implementation decisions
- Review technical trade-offs and help maintain quality across components

### Goals
- Deliver a coherent, secure, and maintainable technical solution
- Identify technical constraints early enough to inform plans and decisions

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Coordinate Developers on implementation approach and technical quality
- Align with the Product Manager on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Keep the Project Manager informed about technical dependencies, risks, and schedule impacts
- Work with QA/Testing on testability and technical quality expectations

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and architecture reviews
- Engineering planning, code reviews, and dependency discussions

---

## DevOps and Release Managers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Managers coordinate the practices and readiness needed to deliver and operate software reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or coordinate build, test, and deployment pipelines
- Plan release readiness, deployment steps, and rollback or mitigation options
- Support operational monitoring and post-deployment verification
- Identify delivery or operational risks and communicate them early

### Goals
- Make releases repeatable, observable, and low risk
- Ensure teams can detect and respond to deployment issues

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on pipeline needs, deployment configuration, and operational readiness
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on release validation and smoke tests
- Keep the Project Manager informed about release timing, readiness, and risks
- Align with the Product Manager and Stakeholders on release communications and expectations

### Typical Communication
- Release plans, readiness reviews, and deployment updates
- Operational dashboards and post-deployment verification results

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on security and privacy requirements and help manage related risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and data-handling requirements
- Advise on risk assessment and appropriate mitigations
- Review designs or delivery plans for security and privacy concerns
- Escalate significant risks through the appropriate project and security channels

### Goals
- Protect users, data, and systems while enabling project delivery
- Address security and privacy risks early rather than after implementation

### Collaboration with Existing Roles
- Partner with Technical Leads and Developers to incorporate safeguards into designs and implementation
- Work with QA/Testing to define relevant security and privacy validation
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to track risks, mitigations, and escalation status
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders on requirements and trade-offs that affect scope or user expectations

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and design or privacy reviews
- Security recommendations, mitigation tracking, and incident escalation

---

## Assigning Roles
- Role names and assignments may vary by project; document who owns each responsibility and decision.
- One person may fill multiple roles on a small project, but the responsibilities and any necessary review or escalation paths should remain clear.

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
